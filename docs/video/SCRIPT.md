## Production notes

- **Opening dive (scenes 0–1).** Google Earth Studio: start at 12,000 km above the Arabian Peninsula, ease to 24.7768781, 46.6170923 at altitude 400 m, 9 seconds, slight bank. Composite the cloud break-through from a stock cloud plate or a Higgsfield "camera descends through clouds, dawn light, volumetric" generation. End the dive on a 1.2 s cross-dissolve to the building render (`tam-building.webp`), with a slow 4% push-in.
- **UI scenes (4–9).** Record real screen captures from the live dashboard at 1920×1080, Chrome at 125% zoom, light theme, with a sample project (not a client's). The animatic shows the exact actions and timings to capture. Add a soft drop shadow and 2% scale-in on each app screen.
- **Voice.** A warm, confident male or female presenter, slightly faster than conversational, with a pause before each step number. Record the lines above as written; keep "The rest is on us" identical in scenes 3 and 10.
- **Music.** Driving, low, electronic; a hit on each step number; drops out under the outro line.
- **Captions.** Burn in the one-line captions from the animatic for silent viewing on phones.
- **Deliverables.** 16:9 master, a 9:16 cut for internal chat (keep scenes 0, 3, 4–9, 10), and a 15-second teaser from scenes 0, 3 and 10.
